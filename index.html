About Us

A community institution, not a project.

Subh-e-Noor Social Initiative strengthens underprivileged rural communities near Lahore — through education, free healthcare, clean water, and zakat and rashan for orphans and widows — rooted at Alnoor Jamia Masjid since 2010.

Mission: Strengthen underprivileged and economically backward rural populations through consistent, persistent development programs — religious and formal education, free medical treatment, clean drinking water, and the delivery of Zakat and monthly rashan to deserving individuals, orphans and widows.

Vision: A society where vulnerable and marginalised groups are empowered, under the continued direction of Mr Khurram Majeed and the wider Subh-e-Noor team.

"You would be more than satisfied to join hands in this humanitarian cause." — Dr Muhammad Hamza Azhar, Finance Director

Our Foundation

Alnoor Jamia Masjid

Alnoor Masjid believes in following and teaching the religion of Islam according to the values found in the Qur'an and the Sunnah — the practice of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ. It is the physical and spiritual home from which every Subh-e-Noor program is run: the school, the hospital, and the community relief effort all sit on the same grounds.

Located at Jhugian Stop, Pandoke — 3km off Raiwind Road, Sue Asal, Lahore — the masjid complex hosts daily prayers, Friday sermons, Qur'an learning circles and community gatherings, alongside the clinics and classrooms built around it.

Education

Formal & Islamic education, side by side

We run two parallel education tracks so children in the community grow with both worldly skills and a grounded understanding of their faith.

01 — FORMAL EDUCATION

School & academic support

Free schooling and academic support for children from low-income families in the surrounding villages, covering core subjects alongside structured classroom learning — reducing the barriers that keep rural children out of school.

02 — ISLAMIC EDUCATION

Qur'an & deen learning

Qur'an memorisation and recitation classes, Islamic studies and character-building instruction rooted in the Qur'an and Sunnah, delivered at the masjid for children and adults from the community.
